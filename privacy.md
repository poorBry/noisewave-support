# NoiseWave Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 3, 2026

NoiseWave ("the app") is developed by Bryan Silinski ("the developer").
This Privacy Policy describes how the app handles user information.

## Summary

NoiseWave does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information. The app operates entirely on your device.

## Information We Do Not Collect

NoiseWave does not collect:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
- Account credentials
- Location data
- Device identifiers used for tracking
- Usage analytics or behavioral data
- Contacts, photos, microphone input, or other device content
- Any data transmitted to remote servers

## Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app saves the following preferences locally on your device only:

- Your custom frequency spectrum
- Selected color theme
- Volume level
- Sleep timer duration

This information never leaves your device, is not transmitted to the
developer, and is not shared with any third parties. It is removed when
you uninstall the app.

## Third-Party Services

NoiseWave does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics
platforms, advertising networks, or external APIs.

## Children's Privacy

NoiseWave does not collect any information from any user, including
children under 13. The app is safe for use by users of all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on
this page with an updated effective date.
